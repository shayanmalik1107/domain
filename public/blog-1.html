How to Choose a Startup Name in 2026

August 12, 2026 • 5 min read

Choosing a name for your startup is one of the most important decisions you'll make. It’s the foundation of your brand, the first thing your customers will see, and a crucial element of your marketing strategy.

1. Keep It Short and Simple

The best domains are concise. Aim for one or two words if possible. Short names are easier to remember, type on mobile devices, and share via word of mouth. If you have to spell out your domain name every time you tell someone about it, it’s probably too complicated.

2. Be Brandable, Not Just Descriptive

While exact-match domains (like "bestshoesonline.com") used to be popular for SEO, modern branding leans towards unique, memorable names (like "Zappos"). A brandable name gives you room to grow and pivot your business in the future.

3. Avoid Hyphens and Numbers

Hyphens and numbers cause confusion. If your domain is "number-1-startup.com", people will inevitably forget the hyphens or wonder if they should spell out "one". Stick to alphabetical characters.

4. Check Availability Across Social Media

Before you commit to a domain name, make sure the corresponding social media handles are available. Consistent branding across your website and social channels is essential for building a recognizable brand.

5. Use AI to Brainstorm

Sometimes you hit a creative wall. That's where tools like the Domny AI Naming Engine come in handy. By simply describing what your business does, AI can generate dozens of brandable ideas that you might not have thought of on your own.
