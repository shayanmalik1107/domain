Understanding SSL/TLS Certificates & HTTPS Protection

August 22, 2026 • 5 min read

SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) and TLS (Transport Layer Security) are cryptographic protocols designed to establish authenticated and encrypted links between networked computers.

1. Why HTTPS is Non-Negotiable

Browsers such as Chrome, Safari, and Firefox display prominent "Not Secure" warnings on websites that transmit unencrypted HTTP traffic. An SSL certificate guarantees that data sent between visitors and your domain remains private.

2. Types of SSL Certificates

  • Domain Validation (DV): Quick, automated validation confirming domain ownership. Ideal for blogs and standard websites.
  • Organization Validation (OV): Verifies company business registration details for enhanced business credibility.
  • Extended Validation (EV): Rigorous background checks for financial institutions and enterprise brands.

3. SEO & Ranking Benefits

Google officially confirmed HTTPS as a positive search engine ranking signal. Secure sites gain a subtle ranking advantage over unencrypted competitors.

4. Free vs Paid SSL Certificates

Services like Let's Encrypt and Cloudflare provide free automated DV SSL certificates. Most modern hosting providers (including Vercel and Netlify) auto-issue SSL certificates for custom domains seamlessly.
