How to Value a Domain Name: Key Factors for Investors

August 19, 2026 • 5 min read

Domain valuation can seem subjective, but experienced domain investors evaluate names using established metrics, market comparisons, and keyword demand.

1. Length and Character Count

Short domain names are consistently more valuable. Single-word dictionary domains, 2-letter, 3-letter, and 4-letter .com names hold intrinsic value due to absolute scarcity.

2. Extension Popularity (.com vs .ai vs others)

The top-level domain dramatically affects liquidity and value. A short name on .com commands the highest premium, followed by tech-focused TLDs like .ai, .io, and .co.

3. Commercial Intent and Search Volume

Keywords that correspond to high-CPC advertising industries (e.g. finance, insurance, law, software) fetch higher valuations than low-monetization terms.

4. Brandability vs Exact-Match Keywords

While exact-match domains describe products directly, abstract brandable names (like Spotify or Canva) are increasingly sought after by funded venture-backed startups.

5. Recent Comparable Sales (Comps)

Investors review historic domain sales databases (like NameBio) to analyze what similar category domains have recently fetched in secondary marketplaces.
