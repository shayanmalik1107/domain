SEO Impact of Domain Names: Keywords vs Brandable Domains

August 23, 2026 • 5 min read

One of the most persistent debates in digital marketing is whether to choose an Exact-Match Keyword Domain (EMD) or a unique Brandable Domain Name.

1. The Evolution of Exact-Match Domains (EMDs)

In the early days of search engines, owning `bestcheapflights.com` guaranteed high Google rankings for that exact query. However, Google released an EMD algorithm update that reduced the ranking weight of low-quality keyword domains.

2. Why Brandable Domains Win Long-Term

Modern search algorithms prioritize brand signals: direct traffic, brand search queries, social media mentions, and high-quality editorial backlinks. A memorable brand name (like Stripe or Airbnb) generates higher click-through rates (CTR) in search results.

3. Partial-Match Domains (PMDs)

Combining a keyword with a unique brand modifier (e.g. `Shopify` or `TechRadar`) strikes a balance between keyword relevance and brand identity.

4. Domain Age and SEO History

When purchasing an existing domain name, check its backlink profile and archive history. A clean historic domain with authoritative links can rank faster than a brand-new registration, provided it wasn't previously used for web spam.
