Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026. Your privacy and search confidentiality are extremely important to us.

1. Information We Collect

Nexus Domains is designed with a privacy-first architecture. We do not require account creation or personal information to perform domain lookups.

  • Domain Queries & AI Prompts: Domain names and text prompts entered into our tool are processed in real-time solely to check WHOIS status or generate name suggestions. We do not store, log, or resell your domain queries.
  • Usage Data: Standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, referring pages) are automatically recorded for security, system performance monitoring, and rate limiting.

2. Cookies and Advertising

We use essential local storage mechanisms (`localStorage`) to remember your local preferences, such as saved history and cookie consent choices.

We partner with Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on users' visits to our site or other sites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • For more information on how Google uses data when you use our website, visit Google's Partner Policy.

3. Third-Party Services

Our service interacts with public domain registry WHOIS and RDAP servers (e.g. Verisign, PKNIC, NIC.AI) to determine domain availability. Queries sent to registry servers contain only the domain name string requested.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption and security protocols to safeguard all communications between your browser and our servers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@nexusdomains.app.
